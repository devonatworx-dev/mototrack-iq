The Services are currently intended for and available to residents of Ontario, Canada, who are 18 years of age or older. See our Terms of Service for details on service availability and territory.

  • To communicate with you about your account, transactions, and support requests.
  • To send marketing or product updates only where you have consented; you can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use information received from Google or Microsoft to advertise to you, to build marketing profiles for third parties, or for any purpose other than providing and improving the user-facing features of the Services described in this Policy.

4. Signing In with Google or Microsoft

You can create an account and sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google or Microsoft account. Google and Microsoft sign-in connect directly with those providers using their standard secure sign-in (OAuth) process. We may offer additional sign-in providers in the future; if we do, this section will apply to them as well.

What we receive from the provider

When you choose to sign in with a provider, that provider asks you to approve sharing a limited set of account details with us. We use:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • a unique user ID assigned by that provider

The provider may also include your profile picture in the details it shares. We do not store or display it.

We do not receive your Google or Microsoft password. We do not ask for access to your contacts, calendar, files, photos, emails, messages, or other content in your provider account.

How we use this information

We use this information only to:

  • create your MotoTrackIQ account, or recognise you when you return
  • sign you in and keep your session secure
  • fill in your account profile (name and email address)
  • contact you about your account
  • prevent fraud and abuse (for example, by matching a provider user ID to the correct account)

Linking to an existing account

If you sign in with a provider and you already have a MotoTrackIQ account, we link the provider login to that existing account only when the email address the provider has verified matches the email address on your MotoTrackIQ account. If the email addresses do not match, we do not link them. This prevents duplicate accounts and protects your account from being accessed by someone else.

Disconnecting a provider

You can stop using a provider to sign in to MotoTrackIQ by emailing info@mototrackiq.com and asking us to disconnect it from your account.

Disconnecting a provider:

  • stops that provider from being used to sign in to MotoTrackIQ
  • does not by itself delete your MotoTrackIQ account, session data, or reports
  • does not delete or change your Google or Microsoft account, which you manage with the provider

To delete your MotoTrackIQ account and the personal information we hold, follow Section 11 (Data Deletion). Revoking MotoTrackIQ's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings also stops new sign-ins from that provider, but it does not automatically delete the data we already hold, so please send us a deletion request as well.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

MotoTrackIQ's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms, we use Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features of MotoTrackIQ, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or transfer it to other apps.

5. Location and GPS Session Data

MotoTrackIQ is a track-day analytics service. We do not continuously track your phone or your location, and we do not collect location data in the background.

The location information we handle is the GPS and related telemetry you upload in a session file (for example, a RaceBox CSV or GPX export). That file may include:

  • GPS coordinates (your racing line on track)
  • speed
  • lap times and sector information
  • G-forces and other session measurements recorded by your lap timer

We use this data to build your reports and maps, compare laps, and generate AI coaching insights. We treat it as your personal information. We do not sell it, we do not use it to advertise to you, and we do not use it to track you off the track or in daily life.

You should only upload session data you have the right to use. Please do not upload another rider's data without their consent.

Uploaded session files, the analysis derived from them, and your PDF reports are kept while your account is active, or until you delete them or ask us to delete them, subject to the retention rules in Section 8.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Services:

  • Service providers — companies that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, including:
    • hosting and infrastructure (Railway and Vercel)
    • email delivery (Google Workspace / Gmail)

The Merchant of Record handles your payment details directly and collects and remits applicable taxes; we do not store full card numbers. Your payment data is subject to Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy, presented at checkout.
  • Affiliate / third-party vendors — if you follow an affiliate link, your interaction is governed by that vendor's own privacy policy.
  • Legal — where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

7. Data Storage and International Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside Canada (including the United States). This includes hosting, email delivery, analytics, AI analysis, payment processing, and the sign-in provider you choose (Google or Microsoft).

Where data is transferred internationally, it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions, which may not be equivalent to Canadian law, and may be accessible to courts and authorities there. We take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected consistent with this Policy and with PIPEDA.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. This includes your account profile, sign-in provider identifiers, session data, reports, and marketing preferences. In particular:

  • Session files and reports are kept while your account is active, or until you delete them or ask us to delete them.
  • Transaction and tax records are generally retained for up to 7 years to meet Canadian tax-record obligations.
  • Disconnected sign-in providers: if you disconnect a provider but keep your account, we stop using that provider to sign you in, and we keep a record of the old provider user ID for up to 90 days for security and fraud prevention, after which it is deleted.
  • Former waitlist records are kept only as described in Section 1 and are deleted within 12 months if no account is created.
  • Backups: after data is deleted from our live systems, copies may remain in secure backups for up to a further 30 days before they are overwritten or purged.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 11). When personal information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including password hashing for email-and-password accounts, encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and secured admin endpoints. To keep you signed in, we store a session token in your browser's local storage; clearing your browser storage removes it from your browser.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, you should also protect that provider account (for example, with a strong password and two-factor authentication).

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or by contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@mototrackiq.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Data Deletion

This section explains how to delete your MotoTrackIQ account and data. It applies whether you signed up with an email address and password or with Google or Microsoft (or any sign-in provider we offer in the future).

How to request deletion of your MotoTrackIQ account and data

  1. Email info@mototrackiq.com from the email address on your MotoTrackIQ account.
  2. Use the subject line Data deletion request.
  3. Tell us you want your account and personal data deleted, and include the email address (and, if you know it, the name) on the account. If you signed in with Google or Microsoft, please tell us which one.

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder. Within 30 days of verifying your request, we will delete or de-identify the personal information we hold about you, including:

  • your account profile (name, email address, phone number if provided, rider profile, plan and promo records)
  • sign-in provider identifiers
  • uploaded session files and the reports and coaching insights we generated from them
  • marketing preferences and any optional testimonials, which we will also take down from our public site

Copies held in secure backups are purged within a further 30 days. Some information may be retained where the law requires it (for example, transaction and tax records for up to 7 years).

We will confirm by email when the deletion has been completed, or explain if we must keep a particular record.

If you only want to disconnect Google or Microsoft

Email info@mototrackiq.com and ask us to disconnect that provider. This is not the same as deleting your account. If you also want your data deleted, please say so clearly in your message, or follow the deletion steps above.

You can also revoke MotoTrackIQ's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings. That prevents new sign-ins from that provider, but it does not automatically delete your MotoTrackIQ account or the data we already hold. Deleting or closing your Google or Microsoft account likewise does not automatically delete your MotoTrackIQ data. Please contact us directly if you want that data removed.

You can control cookies and site storage through your browser settings.
