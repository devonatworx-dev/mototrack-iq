The Services are currently intended for and available to residents of Ontario, Canada. See our Terms of Service for details on service availability and territory.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Services and generate your reports.
  • To process payments, credits, subscriptions, and hardware rentals or sales.
  • To communicate with you about your account, transactions, and support requests.
  • To send marketing or product updates only where you have consented (e.g., waitlist double opt-in); you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services, including in aggregated and de-identified form.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Consent

By creating an account and confirming your email, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this Policy. For marketing emails, we use a double opt-in process and you may withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us. Withdrawing consent may limit our ability to provide certain features.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Services:

  • Service providers — hosting and infrastructure (e.g., Railway, Vercel), email delivery (e.g., Google Workspace/Gmail), and our payment processor, who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Merchant of Record — online payments may be processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record ("MoR") and seller of record for the transaction. The Merchant of Record handles your payment details directly and collects and remits applicable taxes; we do not store full card numbers. Your payment data is subject to Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy, presented at checkout.
  • Affiliate/third-party vendors — if you follow an affiliate link, your interaction is governed by that vendor's own privacy policy.
  • Legal — where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

5. Data Storage & International Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside Canada (including the United States). Where data is transferred internationally, it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions. We take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected consistent with this Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data (see Section 8). Some records are kept longer where required by law — for example, transaction and tax records are generally retained for up to 7 years to meet Canadian tax-record obligations. When personal information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and secured admin endpoints. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications.
  • Request deletion of your account and data, subject to legal retention requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@mototrackiq.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and operate the Services. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Contact our privacy contact at info@mototrackiq.com.
