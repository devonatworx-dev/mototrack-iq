🔧 Setup Guide

1️⃣
Mount Your GoPro
  • Helmet mount (center or side)
  • Tank mount for engine view
  • Bar clamp for handlebar
  • Set to 4K/60fps or 5.3K/30fps
2️⃣
Set Up Racebox
  • Mount to bike (sticky pad)
  • Download Racebox app
  • Pair via Bluetooth
  • Select your track
3️⃣
After Your Session
  • Export GPX from Racebox app
  • Download video from GoPro
  • Upload both to MotoTrack
  • Get your AI analysis!
